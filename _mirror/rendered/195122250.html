请严格按照如下编写步骤：

  1. 创建定时器：gptimer_new_timer(&timer_config, &gptimer);
  2. 注册回调函数：gptimer_register_event_callbacks(gptimer, &cbs, queue);
  3. 使能定时器：gptimer_enable(gptimer);
  4. 设置报警动作：gptimer_set_alarm_action(gptimer, &alarm_config);
  5. 开始定时：gptimer_start(gptimer);
